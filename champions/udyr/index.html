🗺 Lane Priority & Playstyle
Udyr is a Jungle champion. Udyr is a dedicated Jungle specialist with limited flex potential.

Udyr is a stance-switching brawler who swaps between Bear (movement), Tiger (damage), Turtle (sustain), and Phoenix (AoE burn) stances mid-fight. He has very little flex outside jungle, where stance-switching for ganks is core.

How to play Udyr in Patch 26.13

Early Game (Levels 1–6)

Focus on learning Udyr's core ability combo and last-hitting efficiently. Your first item spike at 1,300–1,600 gold is your first power window. Play safe until level 6 unless you have a clear trading advantage.

Mid Game (Items 1–2)

After your first completed item, look to make plays. Udyr transitions well into roaming or side-lane pressure depending on your game state. Track the enemy jungler and contest objectives around Dragon and Herald.

Late Game (Full Build)

At full build, Udyr is a B-tier teamfight presence. Identify your win condition — if you're ahead, force fights. If you're behind, play for picks and objective trades rather than even teamfights.

How to play against Udyr

Against Udyr, prioritize denying their early item timing. Avoid extended trades if they have their full rune set active. Ward against their jungler and track their roam windows carefully.

Pro tip: Udyr's power spikes dramatically after their first item. Play aggressive before 1,300 gold if you're an early-game champion, or play safe and scale if you out-scale them.
