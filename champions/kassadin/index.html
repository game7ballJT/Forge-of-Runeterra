🗺 Lane Priority & Playstyle
Kassadin is a Mid Lane champion. Kassadin is a dedicated Mid Lane specialist with limited flex potential.

Kassadin is a late-game scaling assassin whose Riftwalk blink stacks bonus damage with repeated casts, eventually granting near-unlimited mobility. He has very little flex outside mid, where his mana-shield scaling needs safe farm.

How to play Kassadin in Patch 26.13

Early Game (Levels 1–6)

Focus on learning Kassadin's core ability combo and last-hitting efficiently. Your first item spike at 1,300–1,600 gold is your first power window. Play safe until level 6 unless you have a clear trading advantage.

Mid Game (Items 1–2)

After your first completed item, look to make plays. Kassadin transitions well into roaming or side-lane pressure depending on your game state. Track the enemy jungler and contest objectives around Dragon and Herald.

Late Game (Full Build)

At full build, Kassadin is a B-tier teamfight presence. Identify your win condition — if you're ahead, force fights. If you're behind, play for picks and objective trades rather than even teamfights.

How to play against Kassadin

Against Kassadin, prioritize denying their early item timing. Avoid extended trades if they have their full rune set active. Ward against their jungler and track their roam windows carefully.

Pro tip: Kassadin's power spikes dramatically after their first item. Play aggressive before 1,300 gold if you're an early-game champion, or play safe and scale if you out-scale them.
