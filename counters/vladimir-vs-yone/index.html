Vladimir vs Yone

Top Lane Matchup • Patch 26.13
⚔ How to Beat Yone as Vladimir
To win Vladimir vs Yone in Patch 26.13, exploit Yone's fighter vulnerabilities by itemizing early resistance and denying their power spike timing.

Vladimir (Mage) against Yone (Fighter) is a matchup that depends heavily on level timing, summoner spell cooldowns, and the jungler's ganking priority for Top Lane.

What makes this matchup difficult

Yone presents a challenge for Vladimir primarily because of their fighter toolkit. Understanding their key ability cooldowns and power windows is essential to navigating this lane.

How Vladimir wins this matchup

Vladimir is a sustain mage who pools into Sanguine Pool to dodge damage and heals off his own ability casts via Crimson Pact. His mid-lane version plays near-identically with faster roam access.

Key tip: Against Yone, prioritize your first item spike. At Luden's Tempest, your trading power increases significantly. Force short trades before level 6 if you're an early-game champion.

Item adjustments for this matchup

Your standard Vladimir build (Luden's Tempest) applies here, but consider adjusting your third item slot based on how dominant Yone is in the game.

About your opponent: Yone

Yone is a dual-wielding duelist whose Spirit Cleave applies bonus true damage and whose ultimate pulls in everyone hit along a dash line. His top-lane flex uses the same crit-and-dash combo in a 1v1 lane instead of mid.

Frequently asked: Vladimir vs Yone

Does Vladimir counter Yone?

Vladimir has favourable matchup tools against Yone. Matchup outcomes depend on player execution, item timing, and jungle priority as much as pure champion kit interactions.

What level does Vladimir win this lane?

The first notable power window for Vladimir is levels 3 and 6. At level 3 you have access to your full basic combo. At level 6 your ultimate opens new all-in or escape options depending on your champion archetype.

What summoner spells should I run?

Flash is non-negotiable. Your second summoner depends on your matchup. Against aggressive Yone players, Ignite secures kills in winning trades. Against scaling or defensive opponents, Teleport provides map-wide presence.
