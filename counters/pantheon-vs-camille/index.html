Pantheon vs Camille

Top Lane Matchup • Patch 26.13
⚔ How to Beat Camille as Pantheon
To win Pantheon vs Camille in Patch 26.13, exploit Camille's fighter vulnerabilities by itemizing early resistance and denying their power spike timing.

Pantheon (Fighter) against Camille (Fighter) is a matchup that depends heavily on level timing, summoner spell cooldowns, and the jungler's ganking priority for Top Lane.

What makes this matchup difficult

Camille presents a challenge for Pantheon primarily because of their fighter toolkit. Understanding their key ability cooldowns and power windows is essential to navigating this lane.

How Pantheon wins this matchup

Pantheon is an early-aggression diver who uses Aegis Assault's block to win trades, then snowballs with global Grand Skyfall dives onto overextended targets. His support flex repurposes that same global engage to collapse on bot lane.

Key tip: Against Camille, prioritize your first item spike. At Trinity Force, your trading power increases significantly. Force short trades before level 6 if you're an early-game champion.

About your opponent: Camille

Camille is a duelist who punishes immobile top laners with her true-damage execute, then transitions into a pick-heavy split-pusher. Her jungle flex leans on the same hookshot mobility to gank and isolate targets early.

Frequently asked: Pantheon vs Camille

Does Pantheon counter Camille?

Pantheon has favourable matchup tools against Camille. Matchup outcomes depend on player execution, item timing, and jungle priority as much as pure champion kit interactions.

What level does Pantheon win this lane?

The first notable power window for Pantheon is levels 3 and 6. At level 3 you have access to your full basic combo. At level 6 your ultimate opens new all-in or escape options depending on your champion archetype.

What summoner spells should I run?

Flash is non-negotiable. Your second summoner depends on your matchup. Against aggressive Camille players, Ignite secures kills in winning trades. Against scaling or defensive opponents, Teleport provides map-wide presence.
