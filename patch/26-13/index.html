Champion Buffs — Patch 26.13

Six champions were buffed in Patch 26.13, all targeted at MSI 2026 competitive diversity. Each buff is explained below with its mechanical impact and which builds benefit most.

↑ LeBlanc Buffed
LeBlanc: W cooldown reduced; base damage increased.

LeBlanc's W was the main limiter on her early-game burst pattern. This restores her as a reliable mid-lane carry into melee matchups.

↑ Draven Buffed
Draven: Q cooldown reduced from 18-14s to 16-12s.

Shorter Spinning Axe downtime directly increases his sustained damage output and lane aggression windows.

↑ Kai'Sa Buffed
Kai'Sa: E cooldown reduced at early ranks.

Faster poke and engage pattern makes her more competitive against longer-range ADCs in lane.

Champion Nerfs — Patch 26.13

Six champions received nerfs in Patch 26.13, primarily targeting supports and junglers that were overperforming across skill brackets.

↓ Senna Nerfed
Senna: Soul drop chance on kill: 10%→5%; damage reduction adjusted.

Senna was too efficient at both farming souls and tanking damage. This forces a clearer build and role commitment.

Builds affected: ADC/Support

↓ Bard Nerfed
Bard: Meep damage reduced.

Bard's tank-roam playstyle was providing too much all-round value without a clear weakness.

Builds affected: Support

↓ Brand Nerfed
Brand: Passive detonation damage reduced at early levels.

Early game dominance in bot lane was suppressing too much ADC agency in the 2v2 phase.

Builds affected: Mage/Support

↓ Cassiopeia Nerfed
Cassiopeia: Base health reduced.

Targeted at her laning durability, preserving late-game scaling while creating exploitable early windows.

Builds affected: Mage

↓ K'Sante Nerfed
K'Sante: Physical damage and true damage on Path Maker reduced.

Consistent overperformance in competitive play at high elo.

Builds affected: Tank

↓ Rek'Sai Nerfed
Rek'Sai: Burst damage in extended fights reduced.

Season 2 jungle dominance warranted reduction without removing her early-game identity.

Builds affected: Fighter

System & Item Changes — Patch 26.13

Last Hit Indicators

Now enabled in Normal Draft and Ranked SR.

Previously only in Swiftplay and Co-op. The white indicator on a minion's health bar helps new players land last hits more consistently.

Imperial Mandate

Build path reworked: now Amp Tome × 2 + Bandleglass Mirror.

Smoother component route for support players who found the old Blasting Wand path awkward to navigate.

New Champion: Locke

⭐ New Champion — Locke, the Ashen Exorcist
Locke is a new mid-lane on-hit mage who marks enemies with Soul Nails, rewarding sustained ability and auto-attack combinations over pure burst.

Locke debuts in Patch 26.13 as a Broken Covenant skin launch champion. Expect a 1–2 week adjustment period before their optimal build and tier placement stabilise. Early indications place Locke in the Mage build archetype with on-hit elements.

New champion note: Avoid ranking Locke in your champion pool until Patch 26.13 + 1 when the community has established their optimal build path. Release patches often see overtuned numbers that get hotfixed.

What does Patch 26.13 mean for ranked?

For ranked climbing in Patch 26.13, prioritise the buffed carries (LeBlanc, Draven, Kai'Sa, Aphelios) and avoid the nerfed supports (Senna, Bard, Brand) until their new tier placements stabilise.

The MSI 2026 meta will tighten around whatever survives this patch. Expect professional teams to test the buffed Aphelios and LeBlanc aggressively. In solo queue, the Senna nerfs open up the support role for other enchanters and engage champions that had been crowded out.
